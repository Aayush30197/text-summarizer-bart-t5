SLIDE 1 of 10 • INTRODUCTION

Comparative Evaluation of BART and T5 for Abstractive Text Summarization

🔵 BART-base (~140M Parameters)

Bidirectional encoder + autoregressive sequence-to-sequence decoder. Pre-trained as a denoising autoencoder reconstructing corrupted text.

  • 6 Encoder / 6 Decoder layers (d_model = 768)
  • BPE Tokenizer (50,265 vocabulary)
  • Trained with fp16 mixed precision

🟢 T5-base (~220M Parameters)

Unified text-to-text encoder-decoder framework. Pre-trained on C4 with masked span prediction; conditioned via explicit task prefix.

  • 12 Encoder / 12 Decoder layers (d_model = 768)
  • SentencePiece Tokenizer (32,128 vocabulary)
  • Task Prefix: summarize: (fp32 required)

SLIDE 2 of 10 • MOTIVATION & SCOPE

Four Core Research Questions (RQ1 – RQ4)

🎯 RQ1: Summarization Quality

How do fine-tuned BART-base and T5-base compare in abstractive summary quality when evaluated via standardized ROUGE-1, ROUGE-2, and ROUGE-L metrics under strictly matched training regimes?

🛡️ RQ2: Information Preservation & Factuality

How faithfully do generated summaries preserve core source facts, and what error patterns (extrinsic hallucination, entity confusion, or omissions) emerge most frequently under qualitative evaluation?

⚙️ RQ3: Decoding Parameter Sensitivity

How do beam search constraints (num_beams, length_penalty, and no_repeat_ngram_size) influence informativeness, redundancy, and sentence completion?

⚡ RQ4: Computational Efficiency & Web Viability

How do the models compare in terms of inference latency, peak memory footprint, and feasibility for real-time interactive Gradio web deployment?

📐 Inverted Pyramid & 512-Token Cutoff

News journalism places the essential "who, what, when, where" in the lead paragraphs. Truncating at 512 tokens preserves ~90% of lead factual content while keeping training and inference efficient.

🔒 Data Isolation & Zero Leakage

Indices are serialized to immutable JSON files (data/indices_train.json, etc.). Validation and test sets have 0% overlap with the training partition.

Zero-Shot Baseline Asymmetry

27.87% ROUGE-1

Pure denoising autoencoder. No prior exposure to supervised news summarization or task prompt.

🟢 T5-base (Zero-Shot with "summarize: ")

30.43% ROUGE-1

Multi-task pre-training on C4 included supervised tasks (including CNN/DailyMail prompts).

💡 Key Scientific Takeaway

T5's baseline lead (+2.56 ROUGE-1 points) is an artifact of pre-training corpus composition, not superior intrinsic architecture. Both models gained almost identical fine-tuning increments (+12.4% ROUGE-1) once trained on our 12k dataset!

SLIDE 9 of 10 • SCIENTIFIC RIGOR

Limitations & Threats to Validity

1. Model Scale

Experiments were conducted on base architectures (~140M–220M parameters) rather than large variants (~400M–770M) due to memory constraints on available T4 GPUs.

2. 512-Token Input Truncation

Long news documents were truncated at 512 tokens. While suitable for news inverted-pyramid style, details appearing late in investigative pieces are lost.

3. 1-Epoch Subsetting (12,000 Articles)

Fine-tuning on 12k examples provides rapid adaptation (+12.4% ROUGE gain) within quota, but does not explore asymptotic multi-epoch convergence.

4. Single-Annotator Qualitative Study

Error analysis on 25 articles was strictly double-blinded, but scored by a single evaluator; inter-annotator agreement (Cohen's Kappa) could not be calculated.

SLIDE 10 of 10 • CONCLUSION

Final Synthesis: When to Choose BART vs. T5

🏆 Choose BART-base When:

  • Interactive Latency is Paramount: 0.31s per article (1.84× faster than T5).
  • Fact Coverage is Crucial: Scored 4.24 on human event coverage and 4.88 on factual grounding.
  • Training Budget is Constrained: Completes training in 11.5 minutes with stable fp16 mixed precision.

🏆 Choose T5-base When:

  • Lexical n-gram overlap is prioritized: Leads by +2.65% ROUGE-1 (42.88%) and +2.53% ROUGE-2.
  • Syntactic completion matters: Produces 100-token compound sentences with higher fluency (4.40 vs 4.32).
  • Multi-task unified pipelines: Single model can be shared across translation, QA, and summarization.
